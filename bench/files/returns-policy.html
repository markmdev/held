Returns policy

Time limit

You can return any unworn shoes within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. The 30-day period starts on the date your order is marked as delivered by our courier. Returns requested after this window cannot be accepted.

Condition

To qualify for a return, shoes must be unworn and in the condition you received them, with all tags attached. Please send them back in the original shoebox, placed inside a protective package, as we cannot accept boxes that are damaged or written on.

How to return

To return your shoes, log in to your account, open Orders and select the pair you want to send back. Print the returns label, attach it securely to the original box or a sturdy parcel, then drop it at any pickup point.

Refunds

Once your returned parcel arrives at our warehouse, we'll inspect the shoes and process your refund to the original payment method within 5 working days. You'll receive an email confirmation when it's issued, though your bank may take a few days to show it.
